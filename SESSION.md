## Primary insight

**Interval-Reversal Class (IRC).** After the subtractions above, the remaining unknown is not a missing place, not a missing converter, not a wrong generator, and not a wrong constitutive sense.

It is this: **a published number's interval already contains more than one qualitative world, and the working sentence cites the point estimate as if it contained one.**

Most error bars are innocent. Plants at 375–540 Gt C still dominate. Humans at 0.06 Gt C stay a curiosity. Deep biosphere at 15–23 Gt C is still hundreds of times human carbon. IRC is the subset where the **low end and the high end license different sentences** — majority/minority, comparable/negligible, include/ignore, source/sink — and the sentence that does the work drops the interval.

### Operators

1. **Qualitative-reversal test** — both ends of the sourced range must license different working sentences.
2. **Omitted-interval test** — the load-bearing sentence cites a point or a headline, not the split.
3. **Same-generator graduation** — shrinking *this* number's interval until both ends agree closes the case. Swapping generators is EFC, not IRC.
4. **Fact-freeze** — the inverted number is treated as a fact in theory, policy, or public speech.

**Falsification rule**

- If both ends license the same qualitative sentence → not IRC (plants, humans, uncultured 87–99%, deep biosphere 15–23).
- If the source already leads with the interval and the claim is modal ("may be") → not IRC.
- If a better census of the **same** generator shrinks the interval until both ends agree → the case leaves IRC.
- If more spatial samples at the same precision do not shrink the qualitative split → undersampling or EFC, not a missing place of IRC.
- If locating a converter closes it → TAC.
- If unmixing two aliased quantities closes it → OAC.
- If two instruments disagree → CIC two-clock (Hubble: zeroed).

**Duals:** OAC = two quantities, one number. CIC two-clock = two instruments. EFC = wrong generator. IPC = a cut treated as independent. SBC = wrong constitutive sense. **IRC = one quantity; two worlds inside its interval; one sentence.**

## Story form

A clerk was hired to count a kingdom. She wrote one number on a card and nailed the card to the door.

Behind the door there were two kingdoms. In one, the twilight water held a thin flock of lanterns and the carbon river was a trickle. In the other, the same water was a living constellation, and the river was a season. Both kingdoms fit inside the card.

On land a green thing paused under a vapour named for brains, and the cells of it reorganised as if they had been taught the name. The clerk had been told not to speak of mind. She logged the travelling name instead.

She did not invent a third kingdom. She wrote on the back of the card: *this number is two worlds, and I cited one.*
